Our Credo

"Credo" — from the Latin, meaning "I believe."

A Firm Built on Conviction

Credo Capital was founded on a simple conviction: that finance, done rightly, is an act of service. We help business owners acquire the equipment they need to do their work, grow their operations, and provide for their families and teams. That's no small responsibility — and we treat it accordingly.

In an industry that too often hides behind fine print and shifting quotes, we've built our firm on the opposite principle. Our word is our bond. The terms we quote are the terms we honor. The advice we give is the advice we would give our own family.

"Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men." — Colossians 3:23 — a verse we take seriously in every transaction

What Guides Our Work

Our firm takes its name from the Apostles' Creed — a centuries-old statement of belief. While our work is secular and our clients come from every background and tradition, the character our name demands of us is not optional. We believe in doing honest work, keeping our commitments, telling the truth even when it costs us a deal, and treating every applicant — funded or not — with dignity.

Integrity

We tell you what we can do and what we cannot. We do not overpromise to win your business, and we do not disappear when the work gets hard.

Stewardship

Capital is a tool entrusted to us to deploy wisely. We treat every dollar — yours and our lenders' — with the seriousness it deserves.

Partnership

A financing relationship is a long one. We show up after closing, through the good quarters and the hard ones. Your success is the point.

Who We Serve

Our clients are builders in the most literal sense — contractors, farmers, truckers, landscapers, excavators, and the working families who own and run these businesses. They are people who get up early, keep their word, take care of their crews, and expect their financing partner to do the same.

A Word About Credit

Strong credit makes financing easier, but it is not the only thing we look at. We work with business owners whose stories include seasons of difficulty — bankruptcies, divorces, downturns, hard years. A setback is not a verdict on character. If you are working honestly to build or rebuild, we want to hear from you.
